Research Experience

2021 – now
PhD Researcher · Computational Neuroscience Group, KU Leuven
  • Cross-modality transfer of scalp-EEG foundation models to intracranial (ECoG) recordings with an electrode-aware spatial adapter and dual-stream tokenizer (CORTEG, NeurIPS 2026).
  • Pretrained a large spatiotemporal transformer on EEG with masked autoencoding; scaled pretraining on the VSC Tier-2 HPC; benchmarked EEG foundation models against traditional decoders across BCI tasks (ICLR 2026).
  • 1st place, NeurIPS 2025 EEG Foundation Challenge (cross-task and cross-subject EEG decoding).
  • WaveNet-based auditory EEG regression model, 2nd place in the ICASSP 2023 Auditory EEG Grand Challenge.
  • Online motor-imagery BCI for real-time navigation in Google Street View; binocularly incongruent SSVEP BCI in VR (Meta Quest Pro).
2019 – 2020
Master's Thesis Researcher · KU Leuven, Engineering Technology
